# Round 8 trial plan (from the rethink panel, 2026-11-02 ~10:10 UTC / 5:10 AM EST)

Six framings (HMM/GMM regimes, Hamilton switching, hazard and duration, multi-scale self-similarity, TDA and path signatures, the skeptic) were merged into this plan. Read ROUND8_BRIEF.md first. Every module below follows the brief's contract and uses study8_common.py; the shared helpers the plan asks for (fresh-start mask, onset label, ex-stablecoin target, episodes, episode/block bootstraps, shifted-exogenous null, IAAFT and matched random walks, within-era null, truncation test) are in study8_common.py already.

## What has not been asked

- Effective sample size: fall5_26w (2019-26) has 100 positive weeks but only 12 positive episodes (lag-1 autocorrelation 0.84), and fall10_26w has 27 positive weeks in exactly ONE episode (Oct 2020 - May 2021) and none since 2023, so what AUC can a one-episode era produce by chance (days_since_halving alone scores 0.915 in 2021-22) and which claims are identifiable at all?
- Nowcast versus forecast: the label is 1 through a fall already under way, so does any skill survive on fresh-start weeks (BTC.D within 2 points of its trailing 13-week high: 61% of weeks, base 21% vs 30% otherwise), and is onset or continuation-plus-duration the bot's actual need given it only acts after the 55 gate?
- Is self-similarity in time also self-similarity in magnitude: the three big waterfalls are 2.6-5.5 sigma events at their own scale while the trainable daily population is 1.5-2.5 sigma, so does a grammar learned on dozens of small falls transfer unchanged to the quarter scale (the one test that can support or refute the fractal premise), and does the daily chain's 7-step transition matrix equal the weekly one?
- Is everything above M2-alone era structure: does the five-signal logistic (0.92) survive removing the Oct 2020 - May 2021 episode from evaluation, beat the halving clock and an era-mean decoy within each era, and keep any within-era skill above a within-era shifted null (the thing M2, which moves once per cycle, cannot supply)?
- Vol collapse: BTC.D daily vol fell 5x since 2014 and 13-week vol is now at the 4th percentile, so 5 points is a 1.8-sigma quarter move today versus 0.34 sigma in Dec 2017; is 'no alt season since 2021' partly mechanical rarity, is record-low vol a base or a precursor, and was Jun 2025 (65.7 to 57.8, a 4.7-sigma 13-week move) already the quarter-scale waterfall?
- What does the owner mean by 'ended' and 'how long': a 5.5-point crossing to the 55 gate (9+ events), a 25-point fall from the peak (3 events), windowed (2021 = 133 days) or retrace-defined (2021 = 614 days); no duration answer exists until this is fixed, and which number would change what the bot does?
- Is the entry hazard memoryless, clock-driven (time since the last confirmed trough, 60-1022 day gaps) or covariate-driven (M2 y/y, BTC drawdown as time-varying transition probabilities), and does it rise before the nine small falls, which are the only onset sample large enough to say anything?
- Are the waterfalls one regime: the 2016-17 grind at -0.17 pt/day, the Dec 2017 crash at -0.86 pt/day, and 'crash' (BTC.D falls because BTC falls faster, Jun 2022) versus 'alt season' (alts rise, 2017, 2021) may be two processes that a single active state averages.
- Calibration has never been reported: fixed5's Brier in 2021-22 (0.234) is worse than the base-rate Brier (0.223) in the era holding the only big event, so every model must show Brier versus base by era, not AUC alone.
- Is BTC.D the same object across cycles: TradingView back-computes dominance with today's constituent universe and counts stablecoins in the denominator (USDT.D is 6.9% today, zero in 2017), so should the target be BTC.D ex-stablecoins (65.0 today, not 60.5)?
- Today's reading spans 4% (fixed5), 13% (round 7), 32% (M2 alone) and 35% (BTC drawdown alone) for a 5+ fall within 26 weeks; which of those disagreements is inside the episode-bootstrap noise band, and what would move the reading by how much?
- Is M2 y/y doing work or standing in for two liquidity episodes (2017, 2020-21), is the series the real-time vintage or today's revised one, and would global M2 or the Fed balance sheet substitute?
- Durations: are regime sojourns geometric or humped, are the nine falls' 14-182 days distinguishable from a uniform draw, and does a cross-scale duration law (log duration on log size and starting vol) beat a size-blind band and an IAAFT-surrogate law?
- Is geometric 'instability' anything but realized vol: raw TDA persistence correlates 0.98 with 13-week vol (residual AUC 0.52), and the only new information in path signatures is the ORDER of moves (the USDT.D-to-BTC.D lead-lag area, AUC 0.67-0.69 raw, 0.57-0.63 after vol); is its sign stable across eras and at daily scale?
- Revision and transparency: how much is a live filtered regime reading revised later, and should the owner see per-state transition counts and a revision band instead of a single probability?
- The bot's cost asymmetry between a false alarm and a missed alt season has never been stated; it sets the decision threshold, which AUC does not.
- The fractal engine has said 'final waterfall next' since Feb 2023 (190 weeks) and loses 1.3-4x to no-change; what level or date counts as falsification, written down now?
- Reverse walk-forward (train 2019-26, test 2017-18) as the one available test of whether learned relationships are cycle-specific; and hourly BTC.D or a realized-variance clock as extra rungs for the scale ladder.

## The system of algorithms

The system is a layered reader whose output is a forecast object, not a date. Layer 0 (study8_common, extended by the honest module before anything else runs): data, the contract targets, plus a fresh-start mask (BTC.D within 2 points of its trailing 13-week high), the onset label onset5_26w = fall5_26w AND fresh, a BTC.D-ex-stablecoin target variant (BTC.D / (1 - USDT.D/100)), episode bootstrap and episode leave-one-out over label episodes, 26-week block bootstrap, a shifted-exogenous null (roll the covariate block by 26+ weeks, keep BTC.D), IAAFT and matched heteroskedastic random-walk surrogates, and a truncation test for any filtered series. Layer 1 STATE: the regime module's filtered P(active) from a weekly Markov-switching model on BTC.D's 1-week logit increment with ETH/BTC, realized-vol and BTC-return emissions, and the hazard module's observable BASE/FALL/RECOVERY crossing states. Layer 2 ONSET HAZARD: a Weibull landmark survival curve S(h) for a 5-point and a 10-point crossing with covariates (M2 y/y, BTC drawdown, ETH/BTC momentum, BTC.D vol, USDT.D) and clock terms; the regime module's TVTP entry hazard on M2 and BTC drawdown is the second estimate of the same quantity. The stratified conditional base rate (honest module) is the anchor every curve is compared with. Layer 3 DURATION: semi-Markov sojourn laws across thresholds and the daily duration law (hazard module), the active-state residual dwell (regime module). Layer 4 SCALE: the multiscale module tests whether a sigma-unit grammar trained at 5-21 days transfers unchanged to 91 days; only if it passes does its transferred hazard enter the stack as a within-cycle covariate. Layer 5 ASSEMBLY (forecast module): a pre-registered equal-weight logit mean of {stratified rate, 1 - S(26), TVTP hazard incl. occupancy, fixed5} for P(5+ in 26w) and P(10+ in 26w), with the min-max across specifications as the spread, a 90% episode-bootstrap interval, S(h) for h = 1..52 as the shape, median and 10-90% time to crossing, the duration distribution conditional on a start, and a continuation mode (residual duration, expected depth) that takes over when P(active) > 0.5. Decisions where researchers disagreed: HMM and Hamilton are merged into one numpy module with K = 2 a priori (12 episodes cannot identify a 4-state full-covariance chain; crash-vs-alt-season is reported as a split of active weeks by BTC return sign, not modelled as a state); the observation is the 1-week increment, never the overlapping 13-week change; the headline metric is per-era AUC and Brier-versus-base plus fresh-start AUC, with pooled AUC secondary because era bases are 0.47/0.34/0.08; the contract point targets are scored everywhere, sigma-unit targets are internal to the multiscale module and mapped to points through today's sigma; no stacking weights are learned.

## Trials

### [1] honest (budget 15 min on 4 cores)

**Question.** What is the evidence ceiling of this data and the honest conditional base rate with intervals; is everything above M2-alone era structure, and does anything move inside a cycle?

**Design.** study8_honest.py. FIRST ships the shared additions into study8_common (used by every later module): fresh(P) = BTC.D >= rolling(13, trailing).max() - 2; onset5_26w = fall5_26w & fresh; btcd_exstable = BTC.D/(1-USDT.D/100) with its own fall5_26w; episodes(y) = runs of consecutive 1s; episode_boot(pred, y, n=1000) resampling episodes with their 26-week run-up as blocks; episode_loo(pred, y) = AUC with each positive episode removed, report min and which; block_boot(pred, y, bl=26, n=1000) -> AUC q05/q50/q95; shift_exog(X, cols, k); iaaft(x, rng, it=20); matched_rw(x, rng) = Gaussian walk with BTC.D's bootstrapped trailing 13-week vol path; within_era_null(run, era); truncation_test(fn, n=20) asserting a filtered series recomputed on the panel truncated at t equals the reported value to 1e-9; params_report(k_params, n_episodes). MODEL: pre-registered strata on three point-in-time variables with thresholds as constants in the source: m2_yoy buckets (<0, 0-0.05, >0.05); btc_dd_ath (>= log 0.95, log 0.65..log 0.95, < log 0.65); ethbtc_fromhi52w (>= log 0.90, log 0.70..log 0.90, < log 0.70); optional btcd_vol13w tercile from the training window. At each 13-week refit (expanding, first forecast 2019-01-06, label lag 26; 104 for tte5) count per cell n, k and nEp (training episodes in the cell); merge cells with nEp < 2 into their M2 bucket; rate = (k+1)/(n+4) (Beta(1,3) fixed a priori). Per-stratum Kaplan-Meier on tte5/cens5 (every spell open at the refit is censored there) -> median and 10-90% weeks to the first 5-point fall. COMPARATORS through study8_baselines.run_fixed: fixed5, m2only, m2+ethbtc_fromhi52w, clock (days_since_halving alone), era-mean decoy (fit returns the mean label of the last 104 training weeks), readiness rule, and fixed5 plus the stratified model on the ex-stablecoin target. EVIDENCE CEILING for every comparator and the stratified model: AUC and Brier by era (2019-20, 2021-22, 2023-26) and pooled, Brier compared with the TRAINING-window base-rate Brier at each refit; fresh-start AUC (onset5_26w) beside all-weeks; 26-week block bootstrap of every pooled AUC; episode LOO on the evaluation set (12 episodes; report the drop when Oct 2020 - May 2021 is removed, and when Apr-Jul 2025 is removed); within-era shifted-target null (p per era). CLAIMS, written before running: (A) the stratified rate's pooled AUC lies inside fixed5's block-bootstrap band and its Brier beats base in every era; (B) skeptic wins if the clock or era-mean decoy is within 0.05 AUC of fixed5 in at least two eras AND fixed5's pooled AUC drops below 0.75 with the Oct 2020 - May 2021 episode removed; (C) skeptic wins if no comparator's within-era AUC beats its within-era shifted null p95 in both 2019-20 and 2023-26. DURATION BLOCK: C.events on daily BTC.D at 5, 10, 25 points from 2016 (9/3/3 events); bootstrap 10/50/90% of days beside the same quantiles of 9 draws from uniform(14,182). FALSIFIER BLOCK: the three pre-registered fractal falsifiers evaluated pass/fail/not-yet with numbers (see falsification). TODAY: stratum id, rate, 90% episode-bootstrap interval, nEp behind it, KM median; all comparators side by side; one-step sensitivity (each variable moved to the adjacent bucket; fixed5 with each signal dropped and under +/-1 sd perturbation); weeks_to_decide = weeks of future outcomes needed to separate today's rate from 25% at p 0.05 with one effective observation per 26 weeks. NULLS: shifted targets 200 for the stratified model and 100 per comparator (Pool(4), rng seeded per worker); IAAFT and matched-RW surrogates of BTC.D 50 each with targets rebuilt on the surrogate; threshold perturbation 10 runs with thresholds scaled by U(0.5,1.5), reporting the rank of the pre-registered set. --fast: nulls 20, bootstraps 200, surrogates 10, under 2 minutes. Output results.json {oos, null, today, series (thinned stratified rate), duration, placebo, falsifiers, evidence_ceiling: {blockBoot, episodeLOO, decoys, withinEra, fresh}, notes}; print <= 60 lines.

**Success.** The module succeeds by producing the ceiling, not by beating it: (A) stratified pooled AUC inside fixed5's block-bootstrap band (expected 0.84-0.97) and Brier <= base Brier in all three eras (fixed5 fails this in 2021-22, 0.234 vs 0.223). The result that changes the honest claim: fixed5 keeps pooled AUC >= 0.85 with the Oct 2020 - May 2021 episode removed AND beats the clock and era-mean decoys by >= 0.10 in every era AND clears its within-era shifted null p95 in 2019-20 and 2023-26; then M2 x ETH/BTC is a real two-condition forecaster. Otherwise the honest claim is 'base rate plus readiness, with intervals'.

**Adversary.** Thresholds tuned to outcome (fixed constants plus the perturbation decoy, median reported if the pre-registered set ranks best); intervals too narrow (episode bootstrap printed beside the week bootstrap); era inheritance (clock and era-mean decoys, episode LOO); shifted targets through stratum fitting; IAAFT and matched-RW placebos must be beaten at p95; duration quantiles vs uniform(14,182) to show the law is unidentified; falsifiers with numbers and dates, a not-yet past its date counts as fail; today's spread 4-35% printed as the headline, not any one number. Plus all shared cheap checks.

**Leakage checks.**
- events() peaks are confirmed 13 weeks late: never use peak timing in a stratum; peaks are usable only from peak + 13 weeks.
- tte5 needs label_lag_weeks = 104 (harness default 26 would train on open spells); every spell open at the refit is censored at the refit.
- Stratum thresholds and merge rules are source constants; vol terciles from the training window at each refit.
- Beta(1,3) prior fixed a priori; the base-rate Brier per era uses the training-window rate at the refit, not the era's own rate.
- Bootstraps and episode definitions run after all predictions are frozen and never feed a fit.
- M2 is the revised vintage: reading shown with m2_yoy shifted +/-1 point; BTC.D 2016-18 is back-computed, used as prior only, never as evaluation.
- Partial current week (2026-11-08 bar) flagged provisional and excluded from training.

### [2] multiscale (budget 10 min on 4 cores)

**Question.** Does a scale-free regime grammar learned at 5-21-day scales (dozens of events) transfer unchanged to the 91-day scale and add to M2 + BTC drawdown, or is BTC.D self-similar only in the way any heteroskedastic random walk is?

**Design.** study8_multiscale.py on the DAILY panel; the scale s in days is the only knob, 'weekly' = s = 91 sampled on Sundays. Levels: x = logit BTC.D, p = log BTCUSD, e = log ETHBTC (2016-06+), a = log TOTAL3 - log BTCUSD (2017-07+), u = logit USDT.D (2017+). sigma_s(t) = std of s-bar changes of x over the trailing 8s bars, closed-left, shifted one bar. Features at scale s, all dimensionless, no s, no M2, no halving, no ATH: btcd_r1 = (x_t - x_{t-s})/sigma_s; btcd_r4 = (x_t - x_{t-4s})/(2 sigma_s); btcd_dd = (x_t - max_{4s} x)/range_{4s}; btcd_pos = (x_t - min_{8s} x)/range_{8s}; btcd_vr = log(sigma over 2s / sigma over 8s); btc_dd = (p_t - max_{8s} p)/range_{8s}; btc_r1; set B adds eth_r1, eth_r4, alt_r1, usdt_r1 (each with its own sigma_s). Target fall_k(s) = x falls >= k sigma_s below x_t within 2s bars, k = 2 primary (1.5, 3 reported); mapped to points via points_per_sigma_91 = sigma_91 * P(1-P) * 100 (2.8 points today). Regime layer: 3-state diagonal Gaussian HMM (hmmlearn, numpy EM fallback) on [btcd_r1, btcd_vr, btc_r1, eth_r1] fitted on pooled rows s in {5,7,13,21}, each scale its own sequence (lengths=), training window up to t - 49 days; states labelled FALL/FLAT/RISE by training-window mean btcd_r1 and frozen; filtered posterior by an in-module forward pass; hazard h_m = 1 - alpha_nonFALL (Q^m 1) with Q the non-FALL sub-matrix, m = 1, 2 (13 and 26 weeks at s = 91); E[T] = alpha (I-Q)^{-1} 1. Classifier arm: harness logistic (lam 1) on the 7 or 11 features plus the 3 filtered state probabilities, trained on pooled small-scale rows, z-scored from training rows. Walk-forward: first weekly forecast 2019-01-06, refit every 91 days, daily labels withheld 2s + 7 bars. E0 descriptive (whole history, no fitting): for s in {5,7,13,21,30,45,65,91}, k in {1.5,2,3}: n*s/T, depth/sigma, duration/s, depth-vs-duration slope, relief-rally ratio, mean feature profile over 4s bars before onset, each with 5-95% bands from 100 matched random walks and 50 IAAFT surrogates. E1 within-scale ceiling on s in {5,7,13,21}. E2 TRANSFER (the test): the E1 model applied unchanged (parameters, labels, scaler, logistic weights) at s = 30, 45, 65, 91; at s = 91 on Sunday rows score fall5_26w, fall10_26w (AUC by era, pooled, fresh-start), the sigma target (AUC, Brier), y13 (IC, hit). E3 controls: reverse transfer (fit at s = 91 only); comparators fixed5 and m2+btc_dd_ath through C.walk_forward in-module; stacked = m2 + btc_dd_ath + transferred h_26 for the incremental test; global-sigma normalisation decoy; placebo-fitted HMM applied to real weekly rows. E4 unfolding: depth/duration and relief-rally ratio of the three big events against the small-scale 10-90% band (consistency check, labelled as such). Nulls: shifted daily target panel by >= 182 days, 100 draws, HMM fits cached, labelling + logistic + transfer + scoring rerun; 100 matched RWs and 50 IAAFT surrogates through the entire pipeline (one HMM fit on 2014-18 then walk-forward logistic), Pool(4). --fast: nulls 10, placebos 10, surrogates 5, scales {7,21}, n_iter 20. Output: {oos, ladder, within_scale, reverse, comparators, incremental, null: {shift, placebo, iaaft, decoys}, e0, duration, today (s = 91 features, P(FALL), h13, h26, E[T], sigma_91 in points, whether Jun 2025 was the last FALL visit), series, notes}.

**Success.** Pre-registered: (i) E2 pooled AUC on fall5_26w above BOTH the shifted-target p95 and the matched-RW p95, above 0.5 in each era, fresh-start AUC > 0.55; (ii) stacked minus (m2 + btc_dd_ath) pooled AUC gain >= 0.03 with lower Brier, present within 2021-22 and 2023-26; (iii) ladder AUC flat within 0.10 from s = 21 to 91; (iv) transfer ratio (AUC_E2 - 0.5)/(AUC_E1 - 0.5) >= 0.5. ### [3] regime (budget 15 min on 4 cores)

**Question.** Is the alt-season waterfall a distinct hidden state with a detectable onset and a usable duration, does a covariate-driven entry hazard (TVTP on M2 y/y and BTC drawdown) add forecast content beyond continuation, and does the daily chain's 7-step transition matrix equal the weekly one?

**Design.** study8_regime.py, numpy EM (no hmmlearn: it has no TVTP or regressors; hmmlearn only for the K-grid BIC diagnostic if present). OBSERVATION: y_t = 1-week logit increment of BTC.D (Sunday close); never the overlapping 13-week change. EMISSIONS (V1+): e_t = [y_t, log rv7 (std of daily logit changes over the trailing 7 days through the week-end), d log ETHBTC, weekly log BTC return], diagonal Gaussian per state, standardised with training-window moments. STATES: K = 2 (calm/active) a priori, ordered by sigma of y after every fit; K = 3 only by training-window BIC per refit, logged; K in {1..4} OOS one-week-ahead emission log-likelihood reported as the only metric with ~400 observations. Sticky Dirichlet prior kappa = 5 on the diagonal, chosen by training BIC on the first window and frozen. VARIANTS on one code path: V0 regime-only (y only, fixed P): continuation is the signal; V1 multivariate emissions, fixed P; V2 = V1 + TVTP: P(calm -> active | x_{t-1}) = sigmoid(a + b . [m2_yoy (6-week lag), btc_dd_ath]) with ridge 2 on b, active -> calm constant d (variant: d depends on btcd_fromhi52w); V3 = V2 + state-specific slopes on x_cont = [btcd_fromhi52w, ethbtc_fromhi52w, usdtd_vsma30w] in the mean of y (ridge 1), the dynamics-rule test of the sign-switchers. ESTIMATION: expanding window from 2016-07-03, first forecast 2019-01-06, refit every 13 weeks (~31 refits), EM (forward filter + backward smoother for the E-step; per-state weighted least squares, closed-form P or Newton weighted logistic for TVTP), 3 restarts (deterministic quantile init + 2 perturbations) plus warm start from the previous refit, best by TRAINING log-likelihood; sigma floored 1e-3, transitions 1e-4; parameters frozen between refits; batched over restarts and null draws along a leading axis. READ-OUTS (forward filter only, with the refit at or before t): P(active now); P_enter(h) for h = 4, 13, 26 on the absorbing chain, both including current occupancy and pure entry (active mass zeroed, renormalised); E[T] to absorption; Monte Carlo (2000 regime paths with Gaussian increments, regressors held at x_t, path minimum converted from logit) -> P(fall5_13w), P(fall5_26w), P(fall10_26w) and mean13 = y13_hat; residual duration geometric with mean 1/d (median, 90% interval) and expected further fall in points; transition counts behind every hazard. ABLATIONS through the same harness: GMM-only (sklearn GaussianMixture responsibilities, no dynamics), GMM + counted transitions, vol-only HMM (rv7 alone), rule rv7 > training 80th percentile, momentum-continuation rule (13-week change <= -5), held-out frozen fit (train 2016-2020, filter through 2021-26 with no refit), leaky twin (smoothed probabilities + full-sample parameters, flagged, gap printed). NOWCAST METRICS: detection delay in weeks from each of the nine 5+ events' peak (and the three big ones; only Jan 2021 is OOS, say so) to the first week with filtered P(active) > 0.5, share of the fall still ahead at detection, false-alarm rate (OOS weeks with P(active) > 0.5 outside any fall window and its 8-week run-up); crash-vs-alt-season split of active weeks by sign of the BTC return, reported. FORECAST METRICS: fall5_26w, fall10_26w AUC and Brier by era and pooled; onset5_26w (fresh-start) AUC separately; y13 IC; V3 IC on weeks with P(active) > 0.5 versus V0 with the null run on those weeks; event table of hazard and P(active) in the 26 weeks before each of the 12 falls (Oct 2020 - Jan 2021 specifically, where round 7 read 89-97%, and early-2024 / late-2024 where only small rotations followed). DAILY TWIN (not in --fast): the same specification on daily emissions (daily increment, 5-day rv, daily ETH/BTC, daily BTC return), K = 2, kappa 35, same refit schedule; weekly value = daily filtered posterior on the week's last day; P_d^7 vs P_w Frobenius distance compared with the distance between two half-sample weekly fits and with the distribution from daily IAAFT surrogates; active-state duration quantiles /7 versus weekly; daily-trained P(active) aggregated to Sundays scored on the weekly targets. NULLS: shifted targets 200 (the forecast series is fixed, scoring only); shifted-exogenous 100 (roll [m2_yoy, btc_dd_ath, x_cont] by >= 26 weeks, keep BTC.D and its own emissions; statistic = V2 minus V0 and V3 minus V0 pooled AUC/IC; whole walk rerun, batched); placebo emissions: IAAFT 20 and 26-week block-shuffle 20 through the full walk scored on real targets; two random-walk-difference decoy emissions once, counting refits where the active state's separation loads on decoys. --fast: V0 and V2, K = 2, 2 restarts, 40 EM iterations, 20 nulls, 500 paths, no daily twin, under 2 minutes; the smoke test runs the truncation test at 20 random t. Output {oos (per variant), null, nowcast, events, today (P(active), hazard26 incl/pure, E[T], P(fall5_26w), y13 level, dwell quantiles, transition counts, emission contributions to the log-likelihood ratio vs calm), selfsim (P_d^7 vs P_w), series (P(active), hazard26, P(fall5_26w) thinned), notes (K choice log, W-identity log, in-sample big events, parameter count vs 12 episodes)}.

**Success.** (a) V2 beats V0 on onset5_26w AUC in at least two of three eras and pooled, and the V2-minus-V0 gain beats its shifted-exogenous null at p < 0.05; (b) V2 mean-of-era fall5_26w AUC >= 0.70 with Brier skill > 0 in every era, and fresh-start pooled AUC >= 0.60 above the shifted null p95; (c) V3's IC on weeks with P(active) > 0.5 exceeds V0's with the null run on those weeks, otherwise the slopes revert to state-constant drift; (d) nowcast: median detection delay <= 3 weeks on the falls with at least half still ahead, false-alarm rate < 10% of OOS weeks; (e) K = 2 beats K = 1 on OOS one-week-ahead emission log-likelihood; (f) P_d^7 vs P_w distance below the half-sample weekly distance and below the daily-IAAFT p05. Matching round 7's 0.70 or fixed5's 0.92 is a tie, reported as such; nowcast credit and forecast credit are reported in separate rows.

**Adversary.** Continuation booked as forecasting (fresh-start metrics, momentum-continuation baseline, GMM-only ablation); vol clustering renamed as regimes (vol-only HMM and rv-threshold rule must be beaten on fresh-start AUC and OOS log-likelihood); era base-rate leakage into pooled AUC (mean-of-era headline); smoothed-vs-filtered leak (leaky twin gap published, truncation test); shifted-exogenous null so the regime part cannot carry the null; IAAFT and block-shuffle placebo emissions and a GARCH-like matched series will show 'regimes' too, so finding regimes is not evidence; decoy emissions; K, kappa, seed chosen by training likelihood only with the grid printed; held-out frozen fit shows how much refits learn 2021 after the fact; the a-priori readiness pair was found by looking at all eras (disclosed); label switching checked by jumps of P(active) at refit dates; parameters (V3 ~40) counted against 12 episodes. Plus all shared cheap checks.

**Leakage checks.**
- Forecasts only from the forward filter run to t with frozen parameters; smoothing only inside EM on the training window; --fast asserts truncated-panel filtering equals the reported series at 20 random t to 1e-9.
- Parameters refit every 13 weeks on the expanding window and frozen between refits; warm start only from the previous refit; refits run sequentially in time.

## Falsification of the fractal thesis

The thesis is tested in three pre-registered legs plus calendar falsifiers; none may be re-specified after the run. Leg 1, grammar transfer (multiscale): a sigma-unit regime model trained only at s = 5, 7, 13, 21 days and applied unchanged at s = 91 on Sunday rows must score pooled AUC on fall5_26w above both the shifted-target p95 and the matched-random-walk p95, above 0.5 in every era, with the AUC ladder flat within 0.10 from s = 21 to 91 and transfer ratio (AUC_91 - 0.5)/(AUC_small - 0.5) >= 0.5; structure at daily scale that decays monotonically with s falsifies self-similarity in the sense the thesis needs, and a placebo random walk reaching the same transfer AUC shows the self-similarity is the generic kind any heteroskedastic walk has.
