Open the saved model.json and full-history-model.f64.gz from one asset’s walk artifact. Files stay in your browser.

These spectra describe terminal price direction. They do not yet label sustained moves or executable contract profits. The current predictor gives available curves equal importance; evidence weight and learned importance are different.

Single axes draw a continuous curve; pairs draw a heatmap. Blue is low probability; red is high. Hover for coordinates, effective evidence and the interpolated estimate. Empty cells are gray. Counts are weighted, overlapping historical evidence.
